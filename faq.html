FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Do I need an account for personal printing?

No! Personal customers do not need to log in or register. Simply upload your document on Quick Print, choose specifications, and click Send Order to WhatsApp.

How do I attach my files in WhatsApp?

When you click "Send Order to Winstar", your pre-formatted order details open in WhatsApp. After WhatsApp opens, tap the paperclip icon in WhatsApp to attach your document.

Who qualifies for Wholesale Accounts?

Advertising agencies, graphic designers, corporate offices, schools, institutions, and bulk buyers qualify for our wholesale discount portal with saved delivery addresses.
