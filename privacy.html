LEGAL & DATA PROTECTION

PRIVACY POLICY

How Winstar Digital Printing & Xerox handles and safeguards your uploaded documents and personal information.

1. Document & File Confidentiality

At Winstar Digital Printing & Xerox, we recognize the importance of document privacy. All files uploaded through our online configuration portal or transmitted via our WhatsApp line are utilized solely for printing, binding, or finishing services requested by the customer. We never share, distribute, or retain customer documents for any purpose outside order fulfillment.

2. Account & Business Information

When registering a wholesale or agency account, we collect basic contact information (such as name, company name, phone number, email address, and delivery address) to process orders, facilitate courier delivery, and provide invoices. This information is stored securely and never sold to third parties.

3. Data Security & Storage

We implement reasonable technical and organizational measures to safeguard your personal data and digital files against unauthorized access, loss, or misuse.

4. Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy or your data, please contact us at our store address in Suganya Lodge Complex, Near Bus Stand, Dindigul, or via WhatsApp at 9345046665.
