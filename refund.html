REFUND & CANCELLATION

Our policies regarding quality assurance, reprints, defect resolution, and order cancellations.

1. Quality Assurance & Free Reprints

We take immense pride in delivering top-tier print quality. In the unlikely event that your job suffers from a machine defect, incorrect paper stock, incorrect binding, or color discrepancy caused by our production systems, we will reprint the affected batch free of cost.

2. Reporting Defects

Defects or quality issues must be reported to our customer support within 48 hours of receiving the shipment or store pickup. Please share photos of the issue via WhatsApp at 9345046665 along with your order ID.

3. Order Cancellation Policy

Due to the customized nature of commercial digital printing, orders that have entered production (printing, lamination, or binding) cannot be cancelled or refunded. If you need to cancel or change an order, please contact our team immediately after placing it.

4. Refund Processing Time

Where a monetary refund is approved by management, the amount will be processed back to the original payment method (or UPI account) within 3 to 5 business days.
